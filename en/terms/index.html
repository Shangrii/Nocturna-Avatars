01Work process

Every service begins by opening an official ticket through our designated channels. No requested work is accepted or guaranteed via direct messages without an active ticket. The client provides only visual references, aesthetic preferences, or style guidance; Nocturna Avatars handles the rest. Once the quote is confirmed, a 50% deposit of the total is required to begin the work. The remaining payment is charged at the time of delivery.

02Payments

All prices are handled in United States dollars (USD). We accept PayPal and other methods agreed upon when the ticket is opened. The deposit is non-refundable once work has begun. Payments must be made within 48 hours of the quote confirmation, otherwise the ticket may be cancelled.

03Assets and materials

The availability of a specific avatar or asset is subject to prior evaluation; if it is not viable, similar alternatives will be offered. Nocturna Avatars is not responsible for usage restrictions, licenses, or third-party rights over the materials used. Any avatar or asset that requires usage licenses must be provided by the client in order to work with it.

04Deliveries and files

The Unity project (.unitypackage) is not delivered by default. Standard delivery consists exclusively of the avatar uploaded to the client's account, ready for use in VRChat. The .unitypackage is only delivered if purchased as a catalog extra (Unitypackage, $35). The delivered avatar is for the client's personal use and may not be resold or redistributed.

05Revisions

Each package includes a set number of revisions. A revision covers minor adjustments to the original work. Changes outside the original brief or that alter the scope of the work are quoted separately as a new ticket or add-on. Revisions must be requested within 5 business days of delivery.

06Delivery times

The times listed in each package are estimates in business days starting from the deposit payment. Nocturna Avatars will send periodic updates to the client. In the event of a justified delay, advance notice will be given. The client must respond within a maximum of 48 hours to any request for information or approval; otherwise, the work continues according to the last confirmed brief.

07Cancellations

If the client cancels before work begins, the deposit may be refunded in full or in part at the team's discretion. If the work has already begun, the deposit is non-refundable. Nocturna Avatars reserves the right to cancel a ticket in cases of disrespectful behavior, false information, or breach of these terms, with no obligation to refund.

08Use of completed work

Nocturna Avatars may use images of the finished avatar as work samples on its advertising channels and social media, unless the client explicitly states otherwise when the ticket is opened. The client retains the right to personal use of the delivered avatar.

09Conduct

We reserve the right to refuse or cancel any ticket in response to disrespectful conduct, harassment, or requests that violate our internal policies. Mutual respect is a condition for receiving the service.

10Modifications

Nocturna Avatars may update these terms at any time. Changes will apply to tickets opened after the update date. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

11Account bans and loss of avatars

Nocturna Avatars is not responsible for bans, suspensions, or restrictions applied to the client's VRChat account (or any other platform), whether or not related to the edited or uploaded avatar. There is no obligation to recover, rebuild, or re-deliver avatars lost together with a banned or lost account.

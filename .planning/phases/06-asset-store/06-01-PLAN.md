<objective>

dedicated store page ("Tienda" ES / "Store" EN) and browse Nocturna's products,
rendered entirely from `src/data/store.json` in both languages — with price,
preview image, name + editor credit, NSFW blur-and-reveal, broken-image fallback,

cart (Plan 03) slices build on. After this plan a real user can DO something new:
find and look at the store.

Output: `store.json` seed data, `StorePage`/`ProductCard` components, a small
display-behaviors client module, the `store` route concept wired into routing +
nav + i18n, and a placeholder SVG asset.

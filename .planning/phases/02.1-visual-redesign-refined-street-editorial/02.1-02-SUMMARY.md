  **hero shader** (`glitch.frag`, `shader.ts`), `transitions.css`, `chrome.css`,

  searchlights, chromatic transition, palette retuning). These are visual/CSS-layer changes

  retuned several times via UAT. Final values: paper `#11141f` (cooler dark), on-paper red
  `#c0192c` (brand red). ⚠️ **Open a11y note:** brand red `#c0192c` on `#11141f` is below
  the AA 4.5:1 threshold for small text — the earlier `#ee5a6b` tint existed to satisfy it.
  Flag for the Plan 04 final a11y/responsive sweep.

<objective>
Stand up the motion foundation slice: enable Astro `ClientRouter`, build the lifecycle-aware motion controller (`src/scripts/motion/`), and deliver the first two observable motion increments — Lenis smooth scroll (FX-01) and GSAP/ScrollTrigger + SplitText reveals (FX-02) — wired through the one-clock recipe. This same scaffold is what every later slice mounts on.

This plan also pays the two mandatory, non-optional integration costs of turning on `ClientRouter`: migrating `chrome.ts` off its `DOMContentLoaded` gate so nav/lang/modal survive page swaps (RESEARCH Pitfall 8 / Assumption A3), and guarding the `scroll-behavior: smooth` rule that conflicts with Lenis (RESEARCH State of the Art).

Purpose: Without the controller + lifecycle contract, nothing else in the phase can exist without leaking on navigation. This is the load-bearing scaffold.
Output: `package.json` (pinned `lenis`/`gsap`), the `src/scripts/motion/` module set, `src/styles/motion.css`, `<ClientRouter />` in BaseLayout, a swap-safe `chrome.ts`, and a guarded `global.css`.
</objective>

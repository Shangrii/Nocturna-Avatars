## Problem

Pre-Phase-6 testing feedback (2026-07-07): the Terms & Conditions page needs a liability disclaimer stating that Nocturna Avatars is not responsible for bans on the client's VRChat account — if you lost your avatars (e.g. due to a ban), they are lost ("si perdiste tus avatares, los perdiste").

## Solution

Add a clause to the Terms page covering:
- No responsibility for account bans (VRChat or otherwise) related to edited/uploaded avatars.
- No recovery obligation for avatars lost with a banned/lost account.
Must ship in both ES and EN. Note the standing constraint: the EN Terms translation needs human/legal review before it's considered final.

## Problem

Pre-Phase-6 testing feedback (2026-07-07): on the Home/landing page, the roaming/search lights ("luces ambulantes o de búsqueda") are too subtle — the user wants them stronger/more noticeable, both on the landing page in general and on the hero title. The static/grain effect ("estática") should also be slightly more noticeable.

## Solution

Tune the existing motion/effect layers on Home:
- Increase intensity/opacity/size of the searchlight sweep effect on the landing and hero title.
- Bump the static/film-grain effect slightly (user said "ligeramente" — subtle increase, not dramatic).
Keep within performance constraints (fast load, no burying the conversion path).
